CASE05 · Manufacturing · Forming process

Reducing forming-process downtime and increasing lot weight

A regression baseline links former electricity consumption to downtime and production weight. The analysis evaluates lower idle time and larger production lots as a route to improved energy intensity.

01 · CASE ANALYSIS

Operating challenge

Energy intensity differed between production lines. Equipment conditions, operator practices and product mix needed to be considered before attributing the differences to efficiency.

The proposed actions were to reduce waiting and stopped time, improve time utilization and increase lot/batch weight. Former operating, idle and load time, production weight and electricity were analyzed alongside compressor airflow and electricity.

02 · CASE ANALYSIS

Baseline models

The FM-process model relates electricity to downtime and production weight. A separate airflow-based baseline describes air-compressor energy use. Keeping these boundaries separate avoids confusing production-process changes with compressed-air demand changes.

03 · CASE ANALYSIS

Optimization scenario

The scenario moves daily downtime toward the first-quartile level and daily production weight toward the third-quartile level. It compares 510 with 410 min/day of downtime and 2,778 with 4,100 kg/day of production.

The reported intensity changes from 0.215 to 0.204 kWh/kg, with a reported initial potential of 5.4%. These are scenario estimates, not a completed field-verification result.

04 · CASE ANALYSIS

Measurement & verification

Compare results within the same production line and product mix. Stratify line-specific differences and evaluate monthly savings using daily baseline-minus-actual calculations.

Apply the separate airflow baseline to the compressor system. Changes in production conditions and compressed-air demand must be accounted for when interpreting savings.

05 · CASE ANALYSIS

Engineering lesson

Larger lots can spread fixed or idle energy across more output, while reducing downtime can reduce energy consumed without production. Evaluate both energy intensity and absolute consumption: increased output can improve kWh/kg while total electricity grows.

Future forecasting or anomaly detection would require enough repeatable production and operating data; this case does not establish verified AI performance.

Evidence & interpretation

Model notation and reported statistics follow CASE05. The airflow model requires the original dataset’s flow aggregation and units before operational use. Rounded intensities do not reproduce the reported potential exactly.
