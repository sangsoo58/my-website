CASE43 · Manufacturing · Integrated utility balance

Tracing chiller staging, AHU resistance and compressor losses on one time axis

Chillers, air handling and compressed air are diagnosed as an integrated utility portfolio. Service-specific KPIs and short-interval events reveal operating questions that monthly consumption cannot explain.

01 · CASE ANALYSIS

Integrated rather than isolated diagnosis

For chillers, assess load, power, UPI/COP and water conditions. For AHUs, assess filter differential pressure, outdoor-air dampers and return-air conditions. For compressors, assess flow, pressure, units and loading/unloading.

The objective is to identify whether each utility is matched to actual demand while maintaining production service.

02 · CASE ANALYSIS

Chiller and air-handling observations

Chiller average UPI was 0.84 kWh/RT, COP 4.20 and load ratio 55%. Actual average operating count was 2.1 units versus a load-derived requirement of 1.2.

The 0.9-unit margin is a diagnostic signal requiring reserve, minimum-load and hydraulic review. It is not an immediate instruction to stop one unit. Some AHU filter pressure drops were approximately 19–22 mmAQ versus 12–13 mmAQ elsewhere.

03 · CASE ANALYSIS

Compressed-air events

Monthly average compressed-air UPI was 0.126 kWh/m³ and average system load ratio 81%. Average system loss was approximately 3.1%, while a one-minute maximum reached 31.7%.

Investigate the demand, pressure, active-unit and unloading state at the event. Neither a differential-pressure spread nor an instantaneous maximum is an annual saving rate.

04 · CASE ANALYSIS

Proposed utility portfolio

The source annual utility baseline was 10,403,571 kWh, with 444,832 kWh/year potential, approximately 4.3%, and KRW 49,821,132/year expected benefit.

Cooling accounted for the largest potential. Rounded item values sum to 1 kWh more than the reported aggregate, which is preserved as supplied.

05 · CASE ANALYSIS

Verification and application

Confirm sensor quality and comparable filter grades/airflows before attributing resistance differences to deterioration. Define whether pumps, towers and dryers belong inside each energy boundary.

Verify implemented changes with adjusted cooling load, production, weather, compressed-air demand and operating hours.
