CASE88 · Buildings · Integrated cooling / Weather-adaptive guide

Coordinating cooling start and water/air setpoints with forecast weather

A commercial-building model recommends precooling start, chilled-water, supply-air and condenser-water temperatures together. Historical-weather energy differences are kept distinct from measured plant savings.

01 · CASE ANALYSIS

Treat cooling as one operating strategy

The guide uses hourly outdoor temperature and relative humidity to calculate enthalpy and a cooling-demand index. It recommends precooling start, CHWST, AHU supply-air temperature and tower leaving-water temperature.

The modeled occupied-start target is 08:30. Chillers, AHUs, towers and condenser-water pumps interact, so individual setpoints should not be optimized in isolation.

02 · CASE ANALYSIS

Retain the historical-weather results

The model reads 8,760 hourly Seoul weather observations for 2023. Across 153 summer days, average optimal precooling was 0.95 h/day and mean recommended chilled-water supply temperature was 8.80°C.

Source electricity totals were 531,289 kWh for the existing strategy and 489,695 kWh for the proposed strategy, a difference of 41,594 kWh or 7.83%.

03 · CASE ANALYSIS

State the incomplete energy boundary

Those electricity totals are model calculations, not metered site data. Some precooling-period consumption and auxiliary electricity are not fully included.

The difference therefore cannot be stated as complete net plant savings or verified energy reduction. It is source-model potential under its assumptions.

04 · CASE ANALYSIS

Calibrate and measure the integrated plant

Calibrate weather/setpoint rules against indoor service, operating hours, chiller part-load performance and actual plant measurements. Confirm humidity and rack/zone thermal requirements where applicable.

Include all start-period and occupied-period energy, pump/fan/tower responses and weather/load adjustments in field M&V. Verified saving is adjusted-baseline energy minus actual energy at the same boundary.
