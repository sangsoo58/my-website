Re-matching chilled-water pumps to measured flow and head

Actual chilled-water demand and pump operating points are checked against original sizing. Control changes, impeller adjustment and pump replacement are compared at the required service level.

01 · CASE ANALYSIS

Original size versus current demand

Production, pipework and operating patterns can change after installation. A pump that once matched the design may later operate with unnecessary flow, head or throttling.

The source follow-up action was to select 5°C chilled-water pump specifications suited to actual use.

02 · CASE ANALYSIS

Source design and operating records

Representative design specifications for a 2,000 USRT 5°C chiller system were 150 kW motor, 605 m³/h flow and 60 m head.

Different source operating records show approximately 128.9–133 kW at 630 m³/h, and other records show 134 kW at 859 m³/h or 128.9 kW at 760 m³/h. These are different conditions, not a fabricated before/after pair.

03 · CASE ANALYSIS

Compare the available interventions

Measure flow, suction/discharge pressures and electrical input; locate the operating point relative to pump/system curves and best-efficiency region.

Compare valve/control correction, impeller trim, variable speed, parallel-pump dispatch and replacement. Ideal affinity relations Q ∝ N, H ∝ N² and P ∝ N³ are screening tools, not verified savings.

04 · CASE ANALYSIS

Potential and service verification

The in-progress item considered approximately KRW 70 million/year benefit. A smaller motor by itself is not proof of a successful improvement.

Confirm required flow and head, chilled-water temperature, ΔT, coil service and chiller kW/RT. The final comparison includes chiller and pumping energy.

05 · CASE ANALYSIS

Hydronic-system lesson

Review bypass and low-ΔT causes before assuming that high circulation flow requires a larger pump. Demand-based differential-pressure control may improve existing assets without replacement.

Define the actual service boundary and evaluate lifecycle economics under the site’s demand profile.

Evidence & interpretation

KRW 70 million/year is the source improvement estimate, not a completed measured result. Different flow records are not treated as paired pre/post observations.

Source basis: 5°C chilled-water pump operating records and demand-matched replacement item.
