CASE81 · Data centers · Cooling-tower performance diagnosis

Comparing cooling towers before selecting a temperature-control improvement

Tower performance and instrument consistency are examined separately from setpoint optimization. A poor apparent approach first triggers checks of load, weather, water distribution and fan state.

01 · CASE ANALYSIS

Separate equipment condition from setpoint choice

The source reviews weaker tower performance and seasonal temperature tests. Some other centers were awaiting consistent metering before comparison.

Temperature reset and repair of a weaker tower are different improvement questions and need different evidence.

02 · CASE ANALYSIS

Use matched conditions

Compare entering/leaving water temperatures, outdoor wet-bulb, heat load, water flow, active cells and fan speed. An approach difference at unequal duty does not establish equipment degradation.

Approach is leaving water minus wet-bulb; range is entering minus leaving water. Confirm sensor calibration and synchronized intervals first.

03 · CASE ANALYSIS

Investigate the weaker unit

Inspect fan operation, water distribution, fill/heat-transfer condition and operating overrides when the matched comparison still shows a gap.

A guide can flag abnormal approach and suggest tower/fan inspection; the alert is not a measured saving.

04 · CASE ANALYSIS

Verify repair or reset separately

After maintenance, compare performance under matched conditions. For setpoint changes, include the chiller and pump electricity response.
