Using peak demand and load factor to review data-center electricity contracts

Site electricity and billing records identify a possible mismatch between contract demand and actual peak. The proposed contract change represents cost potential, not a reduction in electricity use.

02 · CASE ANALYSIS

Read the reported contract mismatch

The representative site had contract demand 20,000 kW, billed demand 6,000 kW and a 2016 peak of 4,328 kW. Peak/contract demand was reported as 22%.

IDC load factor was 37%, while basic charges accounted for 31% of the bill. These are different ratios: peak/contract demand does not equal load factor.

03 · CASE ANALYSIS

Evaluate the proposed change

The source evaluates reducing contract demand from 20,000 to 12,000 kW with an expected annual cost saving of KRW 167 million.

A decision must consider future demand, contract terms and operating requirements. The historic peak alone does not establish the minimum feasible contract.

04 · CASE ANALYSIS

Keep the outcome within its evidence boundary

The scenario changes the cost structure. It does not show equipment-efficiency improvement or avoided kWh.

The dataset does not confirm the contract change was completed or the expected amount verified after implementation. Check the actual applicable contract and billing conditions before a new application.
