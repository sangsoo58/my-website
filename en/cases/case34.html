CASE34 · Buildings · BEMS / HVAC

From BEMS monitoring to continuous building performance optimization

A BPOS proposal connects existing BEMS data, design-load review, measurement checks and real-time operating guidance for a supertall mixed-use building. Seasonal optimization is designed as a continuing process.

01 · CASE ANALYSIS

Operating challenge

Building operation can diverge from design intent because of safety margins, commissioning conditions, control behavior, maintenance and changing demand. Monitoring alone does not resolve those gaps.

The proposal uses actual demand, efficiency, control and active-unit counts to give operators actionable guidance and sustain performance improvements.

02 · CASE ANALYSIS

Data foundation

Review zone loads and design conditions; build an equipment-specification database; verify meters, temperature sensors and flowmeters against calibrated instruments. Correct calculations or calibrate/replace instruments where necessary.

Analyze representative one-week windows in January, April, July and October using 5–15-minute BEMS records. Examine consumption, efficiency, load factors, trends and correlations before proposing operating scenarios.

Proposed operating framework
FunctionPurpose
Demand diagnosisCompare schedules and actual operation to find unnecessary running
Performance diagnosisEvaluate equipment efficiency and load factors
Control diagnosisCompare actual control with the intended operating conditions
Unit-staging diagnosisCompare active units with the load-based requirement
Scenario improvementRepeat data analysis, action and result review across seasons

03 · CASE ANALYSIS

BPOS functions

BPOS means Building Performance Optimization System in this case. Proposed functions cover energy, demand, equipment performance, control and unit staging, together with trends, correlations and real-time operating guidance.

The HVAC boundary includes chillers, cooling towers, boilers and heat exchangers; chilled-water, condenser-water, hot-water and domestic-hot-water pumps; AHUs, FCUs, supply/exhaust fans and air conditioners.

04 · CASE ANALYSIS

Implementation & continuous commissioning

The plan allocates 12 months to loads, equipment data, measurement quality, analysis and BPOS deployment, followed by 12 months of seasonal operating optimization. A further year of seasonal aftercare is proposed.

Two activities run together: real-time diagnosis of current operation, and repeated analysis, action and result review to update the operating scenarios themselves. Operator training supports continued improvement.

Proposed schedule
PhaseDurationScope
Foundation12 monthsDesign-load review, equipment DB, measurement quality, analysis and BPOS
Optimization12 monthsSeasonal operating improvement and performance review

05 · CASE ANALYSIS

Target & verification

The annual 3–10% energy-saving figure is a proposal-stage target. The source does not provide a completed, verified annual saving or cost result.

Build a baseline incorporating weather, occupancy/utilization, business hours, HVAC loads and relevant operating conditions. Compare reporting-period actual use against this adjusted baseline so that service changes are distinguished from efficiency improvements.

Evidence & interpretation

The target and schedule are planned activities from a proposal, not a record of completed deployment or achieved savings.

Source basis: BEMS-based BPOS operating-optimization proposal for a supertall mixed-use building; identifying details anonymized.
