CASE49 · Buildings · Design-alternative simulation

Testing eighteen building-design variables against one virtual-office baseline

Single-variable model runs compare envelope, thermal mass, lighting, HVAC and plant choices under consistent assumptions. Results reveal both favorable and unfavorable annual energy-cost changes.

01 · CASE ANALYSIS

Design question

A virtual office baseline allows one variable to change while weather, schedules and other model assumptions remain fixed. The comparison identifies sensitivity rather than promising a universal saving.

Eighteen alternatives covered architectural, lighting, HVAC, plant, control and tariff variables.

02 · CASE ANALYSIS

Four initial comparisons

Wall insulation from 70 to 90 mm produced a near-zero cost change. Floor weight from 105 to 45 kg/m² increased cost by approximately 1.1%.

Lighting density from 30 to 10 W/m² reduced modeled cost from approximately KRW 3,114 million to KRW 2,550 million/year. A distributed chiller configuration reduced cost by approximately 1.6%.

03 · CASE ANALYSIS

Interactions can reverse intuition

AHU heating supply temperature from 29 to 25°C increased modeled cost 5.9%; cooling supply temperature from 12 to 15°C increased it 2.3%. These results require examination of airflow, zone loads and plant interactions.

Daylighting reduced modeled cost by 14.3%, approximately KRW 445 million/year. Its lighting and cooling interactions differ from simply increasing glazing area.

04 · CASE ANALYSIS

Broader scenario portfolio

The source includes thermostat changes, economizer operation, glazing, curtains, outdoor-air conditions, pump variable speed, ice storage and chilled-water temperature. 05 · CASE ANALYSIS

Design decision and evidence

Check lighting service, indoor air quality, comfort, construction feasibility, investment and maintenance alongside modeled energy cost. A cost minimum is not automatically a feasible design.

These results belong to a virtual office, not an actual metered project. Changing a tariff can change cost without changing energy consumption.

Evidence & interpretation

All changes are modeled annual cost changes under the virtual baseline. They are not field savings, current tariffs or universal design recommendations. Signs and rounded source amounts are retained.
