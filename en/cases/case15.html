CASE15 · Manufacturing · CDA and chiller utilities

Building a metering-led utility optimization plan for electronic components

Annual CDA and cooling records support a staged proposal: an initial compressor improvement followed by additional utility optimization. Sensor consistency is a prerequisite for credible performance comparisons.

01 · CASE ANALYSIS

Operating challenge

CDA and chillers together used approximately 80.03 GWh/year. Flow and temperature measurement quality needed to be established before calculating equipment-level savings.

The proposal reviewed demand patterns, air discharge/venting, unit controls, chiller outlet temperatures, staging, pumps, cooling towers and line-level cooling balance.

02 · CASE ANALYSIS

Data and reported performance

CDA used approximately 42.36 GWh/year to produce 245.47 million m³, giving annual UPI around 0.173 kWh/m³. Average compressor staging was 8.3 units, ranging from 7 to 10; average seasonal airflow was approximately 28,000 m³/h.

Three cooling circuits used approximately 37.67 GWh/year. Their reported UPI values were 0.667/0.622/0.706 kWh/RT, loads 70%/70%/52% and average unit counts 4.2/1.9/1.3.

03 · CASE ANALYSIS

Staged improvement proposal

The first compressor step targets 5% against the late-December 2017 average. The monetized second step targets an additional 3% after that initial compressor improvement and an additional 3% in the cooling system.

Install missing equipment flow meters, calibrate existing sensors, implement monitoring and operating scenarios, then train operators and perform data-based optimization. The proposed project lasts about twelve months.

04 · CASE ANALYSIS

Expected economics and M&V

The source estimates approximately KRW 92 million/year from additional compressor optimization and KRW 87 million/year from cooling, rounded to approximately KRW 180 million/year combined.

The proposal states approximately KRW 300 million for system installation/consulting and approximately two years payback on its investment assumptions, including sensor installation. Savings must be verified using calibrated energy, flow, cooling load, pressure, weather and production records.

05 · CASE ANALYSIS

Engineering lesson

Sequential targets must be described in sequence. The initial compressor 5% and later utility 3% are not an additive verified 8% site saving.

Evidence & interpretation

The financial saving concerns the additional 3% stage after the initial compressor improvement. Daily reference values and combined totals follow the source proposal’s rounded assumptions and should not be silently rederived.

Source basis: Electronic-component factory utility optimization system proposal.
