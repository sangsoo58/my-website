CASE63 · Manufacturing · Chiller load redistribution

Loading the efficient chiller through measured hydraulic redistribution

A 13°C section had its most efficient chiller at the lowest load. Chilled-water pump discharge valves redistributed cooling output, and the result was evaluated at the combined system boundary.

01 · CASE ANALYSIS

Selection is not enough

Choosing an efficient unit does not ensure that it receives an efficient share of the load. The source found the best-performing running unit at only 60% load while two other units ran near 95–105%.

This changed the diagnostic question from which unit to start to how cooling demand was distributed.

02 · CASE ANALYSIS

Source unit observations

The three units had intensities 0.417, 0.399 and 0.324 kW/RT, with loads 105%, 95% and 60%. The source labels are retained as anonymized identifiers.

Measure individual flow, ΔT, RT and kW before attributing the difference to equipment condition or hydraulic distribution.

03 · CASE ANALYSIS

Actual adjustment

The completion report specified changing chilled-water pump discharge-valve openings to adjust loading. A staged test allowed the more efficient unit to take additional load.

Individual machine intensity may change as load moves. The final comparison uses total operating power divided by total cooling output.

04 · CASE ANALYSIS

Reported system benefit

System intensity improved by 0.038 kW/RT, from 0.594 to 0.556. Applied to the source reference load of 5,200 RT, this corresponds to 197.6 kW.

The report annualized the result using 8,760 hours/year and historical KRW 59.4/kWh, approximately KRW 102 million/year. This is a report-based annualization, not an independently metered annual saving.

05 · CASE ANALYSIS

Long-term operating rule

Maintain process cooling and manufacturer flow/load limits. Once the load allocation is established, evaluate speed/differential-pressure control to reproduce it with less throttling.

Do not generalize that every chiller should run at maximum load; plant efficiency depends on unit curves, lift and auxiliaries.

Source-value comparisons

Evidence & interpretation

The observed adjustment and report calculation are project evidence. The annual benefit depends on historical load, hours and price; it is not independently verified annual metering or a transferable guarantee.

Source basis: 13°C chiller load-balancing field adjustment and completion-report calculation.
