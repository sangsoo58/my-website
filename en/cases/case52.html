CASE52 · Manufacturing · Compressor staging

Testing the required compressor count with loading and unloading data

Ten-second power, airflow, pressure and operating-state records distinguish useful air production from unloaded running. Stepwise dispatch tests seek efficient base/trim combinations while preserving production pressure.

01 · CASE ANALYSIS

Why running status is insufficient

A compressor can consume electricity while producing little useful air in an unloaded state. Counting running units alone can therefore hide avoidable power.

Large and smaller compressors were analyzed together to separate base-load supply from demand-following trim operation.

02 · CASE ANALYSIS

Align the core measurements

Collect compressor kW, CDA airflow, supply/header pressure, load factor, active units and loading/unloading on one time axis.

Compare system specific energy, electricity divided by delivered air volume. When using kW divided by Nm³/h, the resulting unit is kWh/Nm³; source shorthand must not obscure the time basis.

03 · CASE ANALYSIS

Diagnose transitions and storage

Separate loading, unloading and stopped intervals. Identify additional starts, whether flow actually increased, and how long the added machine remained useful.

Receiver capacity, pressure deadband and sequencing may explain why short peaks trigger unnecessary starts. The lowest average unit count is not enough if terminal pressure becomes unstable.

04 · CASE ANALYSIS

Stepwise dispatch test

Record a baseline, change the running combination during a suitable demand interval, allow stabilization and remeasure total compressor power and service conditions.

Remaining machines may consume more power after one unit stops. Calculate savings from system measurements rather than the stopped unit’s nameplate kW.

05 · CASE ANALYSIS

Verification and transfer

Use comparable airflow, pressure and production conditions or an adjusted baseline where adequate data exist. Establish efficient base/trim roles from actual curves rather than machine size alone.

The source documents field analysis and Test & Adjust logic but does not publish an isolated final saving rate for this case.

Evidence & interpretation

No isolated achieved savings percentage or annual benefit is supplied for this case. Generic unloaded-power assumptions are not used as field measurements. Related CASE25 figures are not reassigned here.
