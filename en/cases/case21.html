CASE21 · Manufacturing · Air, oxygen and nitrogen compression

Designing real-time compressor optimization for a steelworks oxygen plant

A forty-compressor proposal combines gas demand, equipment intensity, load and operating conditions to support efficient-unit selection and required-unit staging across an oxygen-production utility.

01 · CASE ANALYSIS

Operating challenge

Many air, oxygen and nitrogen compressors operated in parallel. Separate equipment readings did not provide a common view of unit efficiency and plant-wide gas-production performance.

Power, airflow, gas supply, pressures, temperatures and cooling conditions needed to be integrated so that low efficiency and excessive staging could be identified in real time.

02 · CASE ANALYSIS

Data and proposed diagnostics

The proposal covers forty compressors and approximately 788 data points including weather. Air-compressor multistage pressure/temperature, cooling water and venting are combined with oxygen/nitrogen compressor power, flow and inlet/discharge conditions.

Oxygen, nitrogen and argon production/supply and gas venting complete the balance. Unit intensity, load-intensity correlations, plant comparisons, trends and reporting support diagnosis.

03 · CASE ANALYSIS

Operating and implementation plan

Choose higher-efficiency units and calculate required operating units from gas demand, unit capacity and performance. Distinguish equipment deterioration from a poor system operating combination.

The schedule allocates six months for system and measurement installation followed by six months for optimization and performance checking. A reference intensity is to be measured after data quality and instrument calibration have been established.

04 · CASE ANALYSIS

Proposal economics and M&V

The source estimates approximately 1,571,731 MWh/year and KRW 110 billion/year of electricity from oxygen output and intensity. Its 3% expectation corresponds to approximately KRW 3.3 billion/year.

Proposed project cost is approximately KRW 1.029 billion: KRW 619 million for software/consulting and KRW 410 million for metering/hardware installation. These are historical proposal assumptions. Verify actual energy and intensity under comparable gas demand and operating conditions.

05 · CASE ANALYSIS

Engineering lesson

An information system provides the basis for optimization; installation alone does not prove energy savings. Establish the baseline and then verify the operating changes.

Evidence & interpretation

Annual consumption, project costs and 3% benefit are proposal estimates, not completed-project M&V results. Their original historical basis is preserved.
