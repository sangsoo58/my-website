CASE56 · Manufacturing · Chiller changeover tests

Setting chiller operating priority from measured changeover tests

Actual operating units are changed while cooling output and power are measured. Specific energy distinguishes efficiency improvement from changes in the amount of cooling delivered.

01 · CASE ANALYSIS

Choose units from actual performance

Similar rated chillers can differ because of age, heat-exchanger condition, refrigerant, sensors and operating lift. Fixed numbering does not establish the most efficient dispatch.

The project used an efficiency-management system and real unit changeovers to test operating priority.

02 · CASE ANALYSIS

The two field tests

On 16 July, changing #10 to #9 reduced kW/RT by 6.6%, but demand increased and total consumption rose. On 17 July, changing #4 to #3 reduced intensity by 15% with approximately 150 kW lower power in the 5°C section.

The numbers label anonymized units in the source. They are project identifiers, not recommendations to operate the same numbered unit elsewhere.

03 · CASE ANALYSIS

Normalize to delivered cooling

Calculate RT from aligned chilled-water flow and supply/return temperatures, then divide measured chiller kW by RT.

Compare load, chilled-water setpoint and condenser-water conditions. A lower intensity can accompany higher total electricity when more cooling is delivered.

04 · CASE ANALYSIS

Build load-dependent priority

Collect low-, medium- and high-load performance curves. Compare feasible unit combinations rather than assuming a single test establishes a permanent ranking.

Include chilled-water pumps, condenser-water pumps and tower fans when progressing from chiller-only to plant-level dispatch.

05 · CASE ANALYSIS

Keep program benefits separate

The broader utility program also reported intensity changing from 1.20 to 0.88 kW/RT, approximately 27%. Multiple measures contributed, so it is not assigned to these two tests alone.

Repeat comparisons after maintenance or changing seasons and preserve production/service constraints.
