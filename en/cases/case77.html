CASE77 · Data centers · Support-facility load inventory

Separating essential and avoidable continuous support loads in data centers

A 2017 consulting scope treats support facilities as a separate load group. Inventory and consumption patterns identify persistent demand that main cooling KPIs may overlook.

01 · CASE ANALYSIS

Start from the documented scope

The source explicitly includes support-facility load lists, capacity and consumption-pattern analysis, improvement proposals and center-level consumption-index diagnosis.

It does not publish a detailed measured inventory of individual support devices, quantities or kW for CASE77.

02 · CASE ANALYSIS

Define continuous load carefully

Always-on load includes both necessary continuous service and potentially avoidable operation. The lowest measured site kW is not equivalent to avoidable base load.

Separate essential support functions from schedule errors, unnecessary running and standby losses using equipment states and circuit measurements.

03 · CASE ANALYSIS

Connect inventory to time-series evidence

Link rated capacity, measured kW/kWh, schedule and operating status on the same time axis. A chronological profile shows when demand occurs; a load-duration curve shows how long levels persist.

Lighting, security, control systems, water services and common-area equipment are application categories, not a confirmed source equipment list.

04 · CASE ANALYSIS

Verify service-preserving changes

Use submetering to identify circuits and trial schedule or sequence changes. Load disaggregation and fault detection can assist, but inferred device demand should be validated.

Document essential service, matched measurement periods and measured electricity. The source supplies diagnosis scope, not an isolated final support-load saving.
