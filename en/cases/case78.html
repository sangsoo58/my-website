01 · CASE ANALYSIS

Use the correct project context

The source describes semiconductor/display manufacturing utilities, including the 5°C and 13°C cooling-water-system labels used in the original records.

Compare individual pump power, operating state, utilization and delivered flow rather than selecting the smallest kW in isolation.

02 · CASE ANALYSIS

Define the performance metric

Flow yield is delivered flow in m³/h divided by power in kW, giving m³/kWh. A higher yield indicates more delivered flow per unit electricity under comparable duty.

Its reciprocal, kWh/m³, is an intensity for which lower is better. Keep the direction explicit in tables and dashboards.

03 · CASE ANALYSIS

Qualify the power estimate

The source calculates power as current × 6.6 × 0.85 × √3, using 6.6 kV and assumed power factor 0.85. With current in amperes this gives kW.

That calculation is an estimate, not direct measured real power. Confirm voltage, power factor, meter quality and timestamps before drawing small efficiency differences.

04 · CASE ANALYSIS

Compare pumps at required hydraulic duty

Match flow, head, valve state and cooling demand; inspect wear, fouling, air, speed and instrument quality when a unit appears weaker.

Required pump count must account for actual system flow and operating constraints rather than simply adding rated capacities. No isolated final saving is reported for this CASE.
