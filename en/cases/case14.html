CASE14 · Manufacturing · Chillers and compressed air

Finding residual utility opportunities in an already efficient semiconductor plant

A proposal examines relationships among utility demand, supply, staging, setpoints and equipment performance after common operating improvements have already been implemented.

01 · CASE ANALYSIS

Operating challenge

The facilities already operated their utilities at a relatively high performance level. Additional opportunities required examining interacting variables rather than relying on a single equipment change or setpoint adjustment.

Demand-supply balance, load sharing, flow, temperature, pressure, valves, control logic and equipment combinations were considered together.

02 · CASE ANALYSIS

Proposal basis and site priorities

Two production sites had combined annual utility costs of approximately KRW 14.04 billion. The proposal estimated approximately KRW 634 million/year, or 4.5%, in further improvements.

Site B was prioritized because its larger utility cost and approximately KRW 476 million/year expected benefit gave it greater project significance. Rollout to other sites was proposed after verifying performance.

03 · CASE ANALYSIS

Optimization approach

The plan begins with field surveys, calibration of existing instruments, supplementary metering and data integration. Monitoring and data-quality validation support real-time performance diagnosis.

Operating measures include matching supply to production demand, balancing parallel equipment, selecting efficient units, reducing unnecessary bypass or concurrent operation, improving weak equipment and optimizing setpoints/control logic within process requirements. Operator training supports continued improvement.

04 · CASE ANALYSIS

Measurement and verification

At the priority site, the cooling proposal estimates 3.0% and KRW 206 million/year, while compressed air estimates 6.7% and KRW 270 million/year. These are pre-implementation expectations.

Verify against baselines incorporating production, cooling/airflow demand, weather and operating units. The report does not establish a confirmed 4.5% field saving or demonstrated AI performance.

05 · CASE ANALYSIS

Engineering lesson

When obvious improvements are exhausted, coordinated operating relationships can still identify opportunities. The proposal must remain distinct from results obtained after implementation.

Evidence & interpretation

Rates, costs and portfolio totals are rounded proposal figures. They are not post-improvement M&V results. Historical contract and investment details are not needed to interpret the technical opportunity.
