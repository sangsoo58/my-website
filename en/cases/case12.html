CASE12 · Manufacturing · Cooling, HVAC and compressed air

Finding utility savings through integrated cooling, HVAC and compressed-air data

Seasonal and one-minute records connect COP, staging, AHU filter pressure and compressor unloading. The report estimates utility savings while keeping production-equipment electricity outside the improvement boundary.

Site
Anonymized semiconductor manufacturer D, Korea

Energy
Electricity

01 · CASE ANALYSIS

Operating challenge

The cooling system ran an average of about 2.1 chillers against 1.2 calculated as required. AHU filter pressure and outdoor-air damper settings varied significantly between equipment and seasons.

Compressor demand, discharge pressure and loading/unloading also varied. One-minute analysis exposed short loss peaks that monthly averages concealed.

02 · CASE ANALYSIS

Data and performance diagnosis

Chiller load, power, COP/UPI, water temperatures/flows, pump/tower staging, bypass valves and header pressure were analyzed alongside AHU filter ΔP, outdoor-air dampers and return temperature/humidity.

Reported cooling-system UPI averaged about 0.84 kWh/RT, COP 4.20 and load 55%. Some filter pressures reached 19–22 mmAQ versus 12–13 mmAQ elsewhere. The additional CDA analysis reported UPI around 0.126 kWh/m³, load 81%, mean loss 3.1% and a maximum instantaneous loss of 31.7%.

03 · CASE ANALYSIS

Proposed improvement portfolio

The main opportunity was cooling-system optimization: required-unit operation, efficient-unit priority and coordinated pumps/towers. Smaller opportunities concerned AHU filters and outdoor-air controls, compressor combinations/pressure and other utilities.

The portfolio estimates 444,832 kWh/year, approximately KRW 49,821,132/year. This represents 4.3% of utility electricity or 1.4% of the whole-site electricity when process equipment is included.

04 · CASE ANALYSIS

Measurement and verification

These are 2018-baseline potential estimates. Build an adjusted baseline using cooling load, production, weather, compressed-air demand and operating hours before comparing implementation results.

A 31.7% instantaneous system-loss peak is a diagnostic observation, not a site annual savings rate.

05 · CASE ANALYSIS

Engineering lesson

Use the same numerator with the correct denominator. The utility-only 4.3% and whole-site 1.4% describe the same estimated saving under different accounting boundaries.

Evidence & interpretation

The displayed item estimates sum to one kWh less than the reported portfolio total. The original total of 444,832 kWh/year is preserved rather than silently recalculated. These figures are potential, not verified savings.

Source basis: Semiconductor utility operating analysis and 2018 energy-savings-potential calculations.
