CASE62 · Manufacturing · Condenser-water temperature tests

Finding a condenser-water operating range through staged temperature tests

Measured chiller intensity is compared at inlet temperatures from 20 to 22.5°C. Seasonal tower settings follow the test while plant-level energy balances remain distinct from the chiller-only minimum.

01 · CASE ANALYSIS

Chiller power and tower power trade off

Lower condenser-water temperature can reduce chiller lift, while obtaining that temperature may increase tower fan power. The objective is low total plant power under reliable operation.

The source treated determination of an efficient condenser-water inlet temperature as a separate optimization activity.

02 · CASE ANALYSIS

Measured temperature-intensity values

The test table covers 20.0, 20.5, 21.0, 21.5, 22.0 and 22.5°C. The lowest reported chiller-only value was 0.567 kW/RT at 20.5°C.

The small difference from 0.568 at 20°C requires consideration of meter precision and natural variation. One observed minimum does not establish a universal exact optimum.

03 · CASE ANALYSIS

Seasonal operating settings

The completion material changed winter operation from 26°C to the source’s 20/17°C setting notation, also used in spring/autumn. Summer settings were adjusted with outdoor wet-bulb temperature.

The public source does not fully define the two 20/17°C values here; they are retained as reported rather than assigning an invented on/off-control meaning.

04 · CASE ANALYSIS

Project benefit and system comparison

Completion material lists approximately KRW 60 million/year cost reduction for the item under historical project conditions. It is a source-reported item estimate/result, not a new independent audit.

Include tower fans and, where applicable, condenser-water pumps in a load-normalized plant comparison. Chiller-only intensity does not demonstrate the same reduction in whole-plant electricity.

05 · CASE ANALYSIS

Build a reliable operating map

Align RT, water flow/temperature, chiller kW, tower kW and wet-bulb. Stabilize each step and observe manufacturer operating limits.

Use load/weather ranges and near-optimal stable bands instead of copying 20.5°C to every machine and season.

Evidence & interpretation

The minimum is chiller-only within the supplied field table, not a universal plant optimum. Historical seasonal notation and item cost reduction are preserved with their limits.
