CASE42 · Manufacturing · CDA, chillers and boilers

Building real-time utility-efficiency guidance across semiconductor and display factories

Five-minute utility data link production-service output to energy input, dispatch and active-unit counts. A single-factory program expands to six factories at two production sites.

01 · CASE ANALYSIS

From a single site to a multi-factory program

CDA, chillers and boilers must provide stable pressure, temperature and flow. The project therefore evaluated energy per unit of required utility service rather than reducing energy regardless of service.

The expanded program compared factory databases and five-minute real-time records.

02 · CASE ANALYSIS

Data and operating hypotheses

UPI, load ratio and actual active-unit count support trend, correlation and cross-factory comparisons. Short intervals expose staging transitions that daily averages can conceal.

Measured equipment efficiency and demand determine which machines should run first and how many should operate.

03 · CASE ANALYSIS

CDA operating-priority result

The source CDA intensity changed from 0.12897 to 0.12627, approximately 2.09% improvement. The source prints kW/Nm³; the original data definition must be checked before dimensional interpretation.

The CDA priority-operation example is reported at approximately KRW 250 million/year benefit. It is an item within the program, not an extra benefit to add again to the program total.

04 · CASE ANALYSIS

Keep completed and potential benefits separate

Eleven applied optimization items are reported at KRW 1.62 billion/year. Three further candidates are estimated at KRW 350 million/year.

The KRW 1.97 billion/year combined portfolio includes both evidence types and must not be labeled wholly verified field savings.

05 · CASE ANALYSIS

Transferable engineering lesson

Define utility-service output, metering boundaries, common auxiliaries and operating constraints before comparing factories. Confirm whether an efficient dispatch rule can be reproduced under comparable demand.

Maintain service pressure, temperature and production quality while documenting the implemented rule and its savings boundary.

Evidence & interpretation

Reported implementation results are retained as source-reported results, not a new independent audit. The three additional candidates remain potential. CASE11 presents the related program from a different technical angle.
