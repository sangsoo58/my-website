CASE45 · Manufacturing · Big-data utility optimization

Finding remaining utility opportunities in an already efficient semiconductor plant

Demand, supply, temperature, pressure, flow, valves and unit dispatch are examined together to find small recurring imbalances after conventional optimization has already been applied.

01 · CASE ANALYSIS

The challenge after obvious waste is removed

The source described an already high-performing utility operation. Repeating generic equipment-replacement recommendations would miss the need to investigate finer operating relationships.

The working question is why comparable utility demand sometimes produces different energy inputs.

02 · CASE ANALYSIS

Read relationships rather than isolated tags

Compare flow with active-unit count, valves with load, setpoints with service demand and equipment combinations with UPI.

Candidate improvements include supply-demand balance, load sharing, setpoint and control-logic changes, efficient equipment priority and maintenance of poor performers.

03 · CASE ANALYSIS

Potential distribution

At site A, potential rates were 9.7% for chiller group A, 3.9% for chiller group B and 3.0% for compressors. At site B, they were 3.0% for chillers and 6.7% for compressors.

A higher potential rate signals a priority for checking assumptions, not a guaranteed achievable saving.

04 · CASE ANALYSIS

Combined proposal economics

The detailed source table gives annual cost KRW 14,044,102 thousand and expected savings KRW 634,042 thousand/year, approximately 4.5%.

The rounded site subtotals are approximately 4.8% and 4.4%. Detailed item savings differ from the reported aggregate by KRW 1 thousand due to source rounding; retain the original aggregate.

05 · CASE ANALYSIS

Make analysis operational

Inventory earlier measures, define service and meter boundaries, find matched-demand performance gaps, investigate controls and turn reproducible improvements into operating rules.

Verify outcomes with a baseline adjusted for production, cooling demand, weather and compressed-air demand. CASE14 discusses the related proposal; it is not an extra independent portfolio.

Evidence & interpretation

All savings quantities are proposal-stage potential. Related CASE14 and CASE45 must not be counted as separate realized savings projects.
