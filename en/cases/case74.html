Designing a multi-site data-center platform for continuous PUE optimization

01 · CASE ANALYSIS

Move from one diagnosis to ongoing management

The proposed platform combines total electricity, IT electricity, cooling electricity and cooling-unit, chiller and tower data across multiple data centers.

Changing IT loads, weather and equipment states mean that a previously identified operating point cannot be assumed to remain optimal.

02 · CASE ANALYSIS

Keep targets separate from results

The first 24-month target was PUE 1.77 → 1.70, with expected annual electricity savings of 6.5 GWh and expected cost savings of approximately KRW 753 million.

These were pre-implementation project targets. The source does not confirm achievement of those quantities.

03 · CASE ANALYSIS

Define the platform layers

Use metering/BMS acquisition, common data definitions and quality checks, KPI/benchmarking, diagnosis/modeling, and optimization/M&V as separately testable layers.

Compare peers with consistent meter boundaries and aggregation periods. Normalize for IT load, weather, cooling architecture and redundancy before ranking sites.

04 · CASE ANALYSIS

Extend automation in stages

Start with monitoring, diagnosis and operator-reviewed recommendations; expand to supervised control only when data quality and service constraints are demonstrated.

Expected-performance models and digital twins are technical extensions. Keep operator override, equipment interlocks and fallback sequences independent of the optimizer.
