CASE71 · Data centers · EC fan retrofit

Evaluating EC fans as a data-center cooling retrofit alternative

A multi-site cooling diagnosis evaluates electronically commutated fans as a retrofit option. Fan selection, control integration and operating economics are treated together.

01 · CASE ANALYSIS

The decision is a retrofit comparison

The source evaluates EC fan adoption as an improvement alternative following data-center diagnosis. It does not confirm an independently completed CASE71 retrofit result.

Compare the existing AC induction motor and variable-speed arrangement with the proposed integrated EC motor/controller under the same required airflow and pressure.

02 · CASE ANALYSIS

Build the operating inventory

Record fan quantity, size, input power, speed, running hours, airflow, filter resistance and cooling-unit state. Nameplate motor efficiency alone does not represent the full operating system.

Identify whether unnecessary airflow or fan-only operation can first be corrected through the existing sequence.

03 · CASE ANALYSIS

Compare feasible equipment and controls

Check physical fit, electrical supply, control interface, fan-array redundancy, alarms, maintainability and the operating envelope. Lower power must still deliver required rack cooling.

Speed affinity relationships provide a theoretical direction. Actual savings require measured fan performance and system resistance, not a direct cube-law percentage.

04 · CASE ANALYSIS

Select and verify a pilot

Develop CAPEX, annual kWh and maintenance scenarios from the operating-hour distribution. Use a pilot to confirm airflow, thermal service, acoustics and measured input power.

Do not transfer the 63.5% pilot result described in related CASE38/46 to CASE71 as an independent achieved result.

The source supports diagnosis and an improvement direction. It does not establish an isolated final energy-saving percentage or annual saving for this CASE. Technical extensions are distinguished from completed field implementation.
