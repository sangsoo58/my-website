2. Key contract conditions

CategoryPrinciple and practical application
Scope and exclusionsThe SOW identifies sites, systems, measurements, dates, analysis services, on-site work and exclusions. Changes require written agreement.
Fee and paymentThe final fee, currency, VAT, payment dates and travel/metering expenses are set out in the quotation and executed contract; published ranges are illustrative.
Schedule and client cooperationProject dates assume access to agreed data, equipment information and responsible staff. Delays in inputs may change the schedule by agreement.
Deliverables and acceptanceList reports, scripts, datasets and meetings in the SOW. Allow a defined review period, written change requests and a mutually agreed correction process.
Data confidentiality & privacyThe client retains its source data. Establish secure transfer, authorized access, retention/deletion, confidentiality and any personal-data obligations before exchanging files.
Ownership and reuseClient-specific data and contract deliverables follow the executed license/ownership clause; pre-existing methods and generic tools remain separately identified.
M&V and savings claimsA model or recommendation does not guarantee energy savings. Verified saving requires a predefined boundary and protocol: Adjusted Baseline minus metered Actual, with suitable adjustments and uncertainty review.
Change, suspension and terminationMaterial changes, additional equipment, delayed inputs and termination settlement require written procedures; dispute resolution follows the executed contract.

4. Measurement & verification principles

Baseline and actual energy

Energy saving = Adjusted Baseline Energy − Actual Energy; evaluated only for comparable operating periods and agreed measurement boundaries.

Evidence levels and limitations

Keep Verified Saving, Saving Potential, Simulation and DEMO separate. Document excluded observations, model uncertainty, atypical events and client approvals.
