BASELINE & MEASUREMENT AND VERIFICATION

Separate operating improvement from changing conditions.

Production, weather and operating hours can differ before and after a project. A baseline estimates expected energy use under the reporting period’s conditions, providing a fairer comparison with actual use.

Energy saving = adjusted baseline energy − actual energy

Define the measurement boundary, relevant variables and adjustment rules before evaluating performance.

See an HDD-adjusted M&V case
01 · ENGINEERING

Understand the process and energy flows.

Review equipment, service requirements, operating conditions and the measurement boundary.

02 · DATA QUALITY

Align production and energy records.

Check meters, units, timestamps and abnormal data before fitting a model.

03 · BASELINE

Model expected consumption.

Use relevant production, load, time and weather variables; assess model performance and applicability.

04 · M&V

Evaluate savings and their limits.

Compare adjusted expectations with measured use, maintaining equivalent service and reporting the evidence clearly.
